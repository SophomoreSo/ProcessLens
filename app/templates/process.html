Process explorer

A directly-follows graph of the selected cases: an edge A → B means B was the next recorded event after A in the same case. It is not a BPMN model and does not show causality, concurrency or decision logic.

Legend

Synthetic start / end markers (not activities)
Activity — label shows occurrences (events)
Edge width ∝ occurrences (frequency) or elapsed time (time mode)
Time mode: elapsed time above half of the maximum
Edge occurrences = total times the transition happened; distinct cases = cases containing it at least once (hover an edge).
Elapsed time = difference between consecutive event timestamps (d = days, h = hours). It can include waiting and work.
Thresholds only hide elements in this drawing; KPIs and other pages are unaffected.
Click an activity or edge to list the matching cases.
